Our Story

From a family kitchen to your table.

Cafeto began with a simple idea: the coffee we grew up drinking deserves the same care as the food we grew up eating. We source green beans directly from smallholder farms, roast them in small batches, and brew them the way our grandmothers insisted — slowly, and with attention.

The kitchen follows the same rule. Arepas are pressed by hand each morning, hogao simmers for hours, and nothing leaves the pass that we wouldn't serve at our own table.

  • Direct-trade beans, roasted in-house every week.
  • Family recipes carried from Colombia, unchanged.
  • A room built for lingering — no rush, ever.
